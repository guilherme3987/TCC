The National High School Exam (ENEM) is the main gateway to higher education in
Brazil, but the most recent results reveal a persistent decline in performance
in Mathematics. International assessments like PISA 2022 confirm this critical
scenario, as only 27\% of Brazilian students reached the basic level of
proficiency in Mathematics, while approximately 1\% achieved high performance.
Research indicates that these difficulties arise not only from knowledge gaps
but also from inadequate cognitive strategies, low reading comprehension of the
statements, and student disengagement. Despite the emphasis of the National
Common Curricular Base (BNCC) on Computational Thinking (CT) as a fundamental
skill for problem-solving, few educational tools effectively integrate CT into
engaging pedagogical strategies. This work presents Cognitivo, a gamified
software based on the four pillars of Computational Thinking (decomposition,
abstraction, pattern recognition, and algorithms) designed to engage students in
preparation for the ENEM math exam. It is expected that the integration between
gamification and computational thinking will stimulate engagement, logical
reasoning, and student autonomy in solving contextualized mathematical problems,
contributing to mitigating the difficulties identified in math education and
offering a tool aligned with the guidelines of the BNCC.
